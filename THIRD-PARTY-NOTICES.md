RCSB PDB data files are available under the CC0 1.0 public domain dedication (https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy).

## Code written for this extension

The PDB, mmCIF, CIF, PQR, SDF, MOL2, XYZ and GRO readers, the tree and sequence views, the geometry for measurements, hydrogen bond candidates, contacts and binding sites, the sequence alignment and the superposition (Horn's quaternion method, B.
