# Styles, colors and atom details

- Style: cartoon, ball and stick, sticks, spheres or lines. Ligands stay as ball and stick next to a cartoon.
- Color: by chain, residue type, element, B-factor (blue low, red high) or secondary structure.
- Surface draws a molecular surface over the protein. Large structures take a few seconds.
- Click an atom for its name, element, residue, chain, serial number, coordinates and B-factor.
- Click a residue in the tree or a letter in the sequence strip to zoom to it.
- Files with several models or frames get a Model picker in the toolbar.
